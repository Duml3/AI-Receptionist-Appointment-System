Privacy Policy

AI Receptionist Appointment System

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the AI Receptionist Appointment System collects, uses, stores, and protects information when users interact with the application and its connected services.

The AI Receptionist Appointment System is an AI-powered receptionist and appointment management system designed to assist businesses with customer communication, appointment scheduling, customer management, and related administrative workflows.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how the application is used, the system may process information necessary to provide its functionality, including:

  • Customer name and contact information
  • Telephone number
  • Email address
  • Appointment information
  • Customer requests and messages
  • Conversation information necessary to provide receptionist services
  • Customer-specific preferences that are explicitly provided
  • Information required to manage appointment availability and bookings

2. Google User Data

When the application uses authorized Google services, it may access Google user data that is necessary for the specific functionality requested by the application.

Depending on the Google services and OAuth scopes authorized by the user, this may include information required to interact with authorized Google services such as calendar information.

The application accesses Google user data only for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy and for providing the functionality requested by the user.

The application does not sell Google user data.

Google user data is not used for targeted advertising, personalized advertising, or data brokerage.

Google user data is not used to develop, improve, or train generalized or non-personalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed by the application may be used to:

  • Provide AI receptionist functionality
  • Respond to customer requests
  • Check appointment availability
  • Assist with appointment scheduling
  • Manage customer information
  • Maintain relevant conversation records
  • Store explicitly provided customer preferences when appropriate
  • Provide business-related information
  • Operate and maintain the application's workflows
  • Improve the reliability and functionality of the application

4. AI and Automated Processing

The application uses artificial intelligence and automated workflows to provide receptionist and appointment-management functionality.

AI-generated responses are based on approved business information, authorized application data, and information provided during the customer's interaction.

The application is designed not to intentionally expose private customer information to other customers or unauthorized users.

5. Information Sharing

Information may be processed by service providers and technology platforms that are necessary to operate the application.

These services may include AI, workflow automation, appointment management, database, hosting, and authentication services.

Information is not sold to data brokers or used for targeted advertising.

Information may be disclosed when required by law or when necessary to protect the security, rights, or operation of the application.

6. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security. Users should therefore understand that information transmitted through online services may carry inherent security risks.

7. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the application's services, maintain operational records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce applicable agreements.

8. User Rights and Requests

Users may request information about personal data processed by the application and may request correction or deletion where applicable.

Requests should be submitted to the application owner or administrator responsible for the service.

9. Third-Party Services

The application may integrate with third-party services to provide functionality such as AI communication, workflow automation, appointment scheduling, authentication, and data storage.

Third-party services may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's functionality, data practices, or applicable requirements change.

The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

11. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the application's data practices, please contact the application administrator.
